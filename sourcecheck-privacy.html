SourceCheck — Privacy Policy

Effective 28 November 2026 · Published by Arsy Labs

SourceCheck is an Atlassian Forge app that audits Confluence documentation health: page staleness, factual claims without sources, and conflicts between a page's stated status and the status of its linked Jira issues. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data we access

When a user invokes one of SourceCheck's actions (in Rovo Chat, through a Rovo Studio agent, or through the MCP server), the app reads, through the Atlassian APIs and only with the permissions the installer granted:

  • the content and version metadata of Confluence pages the app user is permitted to view, and
  • the status of Jira issues linked from those pages.

Data we store

SourceCheck stores no personal data and no page content. All processing happens in memory during a single request; audit results are returned to the caller and are not retained, logged, or transmitted anywhere else by the app. The app operates no external servers and keeps no database.

Data sharing

No data accessed by SourceCheck is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling.

Platform data

Atlassian may process data according to Atlassian's own privacy policy for the Forge platform and the Atlassian products the app is installed on. That processing is outside our control.
